# Flywheel (manual, dual-mass)
Photos used: refs/flywheel/flywheel-1.webp (M40 diagram: ring gear + hub bolt circle, flat ring teeth; single-mass, so only
for gear/hub layout), flywheel-2.jpg (Turner DMF, secondary-mass face). Catalog photo-set "Flywheel, starter, rear face":
images 12-13 are the manual references; image 14 (flexplate) deliberately NOT used.
- DMF: primary mass bolts to crank flange; ring gear welded on the primary rim; a visible seam/gap, then the secondary mass
  whose face is a flat polished ring (clutch friction surface) with a recessed centre showing crank-bolt access holes and
  the centre bearing. Face ring carries clutch-cover threaded holes + 2-3 dowel pins.
- Model: lathe primary plate (r37.5), ring gear r38.5 + 120 instanced teeth (tip r40.5, real is ~135), secondary mass r35
  with recess r21, 6 crank bolts on the r17 circle (matches crankshaft.js flange: 6 bolts), 6 cover holes r29, 3 dowels r31.
- Group origin = crank rear flange face (x=-LEN/2); ring-gear plane is 8 behind it (starter pinion x matches).
